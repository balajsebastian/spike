# Privacy Policy — Spike

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

## Overview

Spike is a personal glucose monitoring helper that displays readings from your CGM system (Dexcom Share, LibreLinkUp, or Nightscout). Your privacy is simple: we don't collect anything.

## Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or health information.

## How the app works

- Your credentials (Dexcom, LibreLinkUp, or Nightscout) are stored locally on your device using the operating system's secure keychain. They never leave your phone.
- Glucose data is fetched directly from your CGM provider's servers to your device. No intermediary servers are involved.
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind is included in the app.

## Third-party services

The app communicates directly with the following services depending on your chosen CGM source:

- **Dexcom Share** (share2.dexcom.com / shareous1.dexcom.com)
- **LibreLinkUp** (api.libreview.io and regional variants)
- **Nightscout** (your self-hosted instance)

These communications are governed by each provider's own privacy policy and terms of service.

## Data deletion

To remove all stored data, simply log out within the app or uninstall it. All locally stored credentials are deleted immediately.
